# Privacy Policy — Whoop Insights

_Last updated: 2026-06-04_

**Whoop Insights** is a personal, single-user application built and operated by
its owner for their own use. It is not a commercial product and has no other
users.

## What data it accesses

With your authorization via WHOOP's OAuth 2.0 flow, the app reads your own WHOOP
data — physiological cycles, recovery, sleep, and workouts, plus basic profile
and body measurements — using the WHOOP API v2.

## How data is stored and used

- Data is fetched and stored in a private database (Supabase Postgres) that the
  app owner controls.
- It is used solely to display the owner's own trends, comparisons, and
  insights within the app.
- The app runs locally and/or in the owner's own private deployment.

## What is **not** done with the data

- It is **not** sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.
- It is **not** used for advertising, profiling for others, or any commercial
  purpose.
- No other person has access to the data through this application.

## Authorization and revocation

Access is granted by you through WHOOP's OAuth flow and can be revoked at any
time from your WHOOP account's connected-apps settings or by deleting the
application's stored tokens. Revoking access stops further data syncing.
